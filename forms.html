What's new in the DRC v2 forms?

Both forms retain their existing Kobo form IDs and use version 20261117a. The shared assessor/date and school blocks capture province, level, stable school key, identifiers and data-quality corrections. The Assessment adds a top-three priority-support selection and calculates a provisional score. The Conditions questionnaire splits land and operating documents and removes duplicate asset questions.

For implementation: previous test exports may have different variable names; use the versioned field catalogue and do not join old submissions by position. The school-to-office mapping and final rating rules must be agreed with DRC.
