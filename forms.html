The School Assessment and School Evaluation serve related but different purposes. They must remain separate in collection and interpretation, while approved school identifiers may help link their results in a future reporting system.

Prototype status. Both forms are working drafts. This page contains their design summaries, not a submission portal. No live KoboToolbox records, approvals, assessment results, or compliance decisions are shown here.
Form 01 · School conditions

School Assessment

Working prototype · field testing pending

Eight source-defined criteria. Captures infrastructure, capacity and the resources schools need, with provisional 1–4 ratings that are not an approved weighted score.

8criteria
2inventory repeats
155possible response fields*
1 · Infrastructure and WASH

Classrooms and condition; sanitation, handwashing and drinking water

2 · Equipment, furniture and teaching materials

One inventory entry per category or title, not per physical item; includes quantity and condition checks

3 · Enrolment and capacity

Enrollment, attendance and available school capacity

4 · Competitiveness and access

Alternative schools, walking time and school-choice context

5 · Governance and administration

School management roles and records

6 · Community involvement

School committees and parent participation

7 · Fee payment

Fee policy, payment patterns and supporting evidence

8 · External support

Government, community, private and other support

*The count describes potential XLSForm response fields; skip logic means an assessor will not see every question.
